How the days work

Two shoot days a month. On your calendar, at your office.

We come to you. Office, or a location of your choice. One camera operator, one assistant. We bring a shot list built from a short call with you beforehand, so there's nothing to prepare and nothing to memorize.

Across the two days, you talk through your business, your opinions, your customers, your wins. We shoot multicam throughout, an FX3 on your main angle and a ZV-E10 II on a wide, so every piece cuts between two angles instead of sitting flat on one shot.

We capture enough setups to cut a full month of content from, without cramming everything into a single exhausting day.

Why two days, not one
One long day burns founders out and shows in the footage. Two spaced-out days mean fresher energy, better takes, and room to change your mind about what to cover after seeing the first cut.
